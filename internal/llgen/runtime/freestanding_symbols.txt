# (or a hosted target with `--sys none`) archive may leave for the
# embedder: libc calls rt_core.c makes directly, plus the rt_sys_*
# boundary points (rt.h) an embedder may override with real ones. A new
# runtime libc call or a changed rt_sys_* boundary must land here too, or
# internal/driver's TestBuildFreestandingSymbolSet fails naming it.
# memcpy, memmove, memset and memcmp are also what the compiler itself calls for a large copy or compare.
